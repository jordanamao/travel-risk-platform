      Glossary

      METARStands for Meteorological Aerodrome Report. The app uses METAR in airport weather evidence and airport risk signals to read live visibility, clouds, wind, gusts, and VFR/MVFR/IFR status.

      VFRVisual Flight Rules. Good flying conditions. Pilots can generally see clearly enough to fly by visual reference.

      MVFRMarginal Visual Flight Rules. Reduced visibility or lower clouds. Conditions are not severe, but they require more caution because they are getting worse.

      IFRInstrument Flight Rules. Poor visibility or low clouds. Pilots rely more on instruments, and delays or cancellations are more likely.

      FAAFederal Aviation Administration. In this app, FAA data means live U.S. airport operational status such as ground stops, delay programs, arrival/departure delays, and closures.

      NASNational Airspace System. The FAA NAS status feed reports current airport and airspace disruptions that can affect flight operations.

      NWSNational Weather Service official U.S. weather alerts and forecasts.

      Precipitation probabilityChance that measurable rain or snow occurs during the forecast period.

      GustWind is the steady speed. Gust is a short stronger burst. Gusts matter because sudden crosswinds can affect takeoff, landing, and high-profile vehicles.
