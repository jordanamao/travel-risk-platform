      Glossary Definitions for the risk and aviation terms used in the report.
      Flight conditions
      VFR
      Visual Flight Rules: clear enough to fly by sight. Good conditions.
      MVFR
      Marginal VFR: lower clouds or reduced visibility, so more caution is needed.
      IFR
      Instrument Flight Rules: poor visibility or low clouds; delays are more likely.
      LIFR
      Low IFR: very poor visibility or very low clouds; the highest disruption risk.
      Data sources
      METAR
      Live airport weather report: visibility, clouds, wind, and flight category.
      FAA / NAS
      FAA status for the National Airspace System: ground stops, delay programs, and closures.
      NWS
      National Weather Service official U.S. forecasts and alerts.
      511 road data
      State traffic feeds of current road closures, work zones, and incidents.
      Weather terms
      Precipitation probability
      Chance of measurable rain or snow during the forecast period.
      Gust
      A short burst of wind above the steady speed; matters for takeoff, landing, and tall vehicles.
      How the score works
      Signal
      A risky finding that adds points to the score.
      Evidence
      Everything a source returned, including low-risk data that adds no points.
      Risk score
      High signals add 6 points, Medium 3, Low 1. 10+ points is High, 5 to 9 is Medium.
      Confidence
      How much independent evidence backs the score; more agreeing signals means higher confidence.
